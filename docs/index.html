How it works

We set this up as a dynamic program, the same way we did in class.

  • Stages: each possession left in the game is one stage. You and the other team take turns, and you have the ball now.
  • State: the situation at your 4th down. That is the score, where the ball is, and yards to go.
  • Decision: go for it, kick a field goal, or punt.
  • Goal: we work backward from the end of the game. A win counts as 1, a tie as 0.5, and a loss as 0. At every 4th down we pick the call with the highest win chance.

The chances come from real games: how often teams convert, how often kicks are good, how far punts go, and how drives usually end. Both teams are assumed to play like an average NFL team the rest of the time.

The decision tree

The square is your call. Circles are chance, like converting or not. Each triangle shows your win chance at the end. The two crossed-out branches are the calls we did not pick.
