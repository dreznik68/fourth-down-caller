4th Down Caller: how to use it

Go for it, kick a field goal, or punt? The tool gives the call with the best chance to win, from a probabilistic dynamic program built on 2018 to 2025 NFL regular season play-by-play.

Open it: dreznik68.github.io/fourth-down-caller (works on a phone, no sign-up)

Scan to open

Four steps

  1. Possessions left. Count this possession as 1. Not sure? Pick the minutes left in the 4th quarter under "or from the clock" and the tool fills in the typical number (about 5 with 10 minutes left, 3 with 5 minutes, 2 with 2 minutes).
  2. Score. Your points minus theirs (for example, "Down 3").
  3. Field position. Where the ball is, in 5-yard groups. "Own 31-35" is your side of the field, "Opp 30-34" is theirs. The tool works out each group at its spot closest to the opponent's goal.
  4. Yards to go. 1 to 9, or 10+.

Reading the answer

Best call is in large text, with its chance to win. Under it are the win probabilities for all three calls, and a one-line Why?.

Decision tree: the square is your decision, each circle is a chance outcome (convert or fail, kick good or missed) with its probability on the branch, and each triangle shows the win probability at the end. Each call's win probability is the outcome chances times the win probability at each end. The calls not taken are crossed out. The punt has no circle because the model uses the average punt from that spot.

Best call for each yards to go: the strip shows the best call at your field position for every yards to go, so you can see where the answer flips. A dot marks a close call (within 1 point of win probability). Tap a box to switch to it.

Three worked examples

Example 1: Tied, 4th and 1, your own 41 to 45

Enter: 4 possessions left (about 6 to 8 minutes left), Tied, your own 41 to 45, 1 yard to go.

Best call: Go for it, by 6.9 points.

Win probability: go for it 49.4%, punt 42.5%, field goal 28.6%.

Example 2: Down 3, 4th and 6, opponent's 30 to 34

Enter: 2 possessions left (about 2 minutes left), Down 3, opponent's 30 to 34, 6 yards to go.

Best call: Field goal, by 2.5 points.

Win probability: field goal 22.7%, go for it 20.1%, punt 0.0%.

Example 3: Up 3, 4th and 4, your own 31 to 35

Enter: 2 possessions left (about 2 minutes left), Up 3, your own 31 to 35, 4 yards to go.

Best call: Punt, by 7.5 points.

Win probability: punt 70.8%, go for it 63.2%, field goal 37.2%.

What the model assumes

One fourth-down decision per possession. Both teams otherwise play like a typical NFL team. A punt goes the average net distance for that spot. A new possession (after a score or a kickoff) starts at your own 30. The number of possessions left is known. A tie after the last possession counts as half a win (standing in for overtime). Win probabilities come from league-wide data, not from the two teams playing.
