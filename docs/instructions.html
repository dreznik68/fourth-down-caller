How to use the 4th Down Caller

It tells you whether to go for it, kick a field goal, or punt. It picks the call that gives you the best chance to win. We built it from NFL play-by-play data (2018 to 2025 regular seasons).

Open it: dreznik68.github.io/fourth-down-caller. It works on your phone and there is no sign-up.

Scan to open

Four steps

  1. Possessions left. Count the one you are on now as 1. Not sure? Pick the minutes left under "Or use the clock" and it fills in a typical number. Roughly: 5 with 10 minutes left, 3 with 5, and 2 with 2.
  2. Score. Your points minus theirs, like "Down 3".
  3. Field position. Pick the 5-yard range the ball is in. "Own 31-35" is your side of the field and "Opp 30-34" is theirs. The model uses the yard line in that range closest to the end zone you are going for.
  4. Yards to go. 1 to 9, or 10+.

Reading the answer

Best call: the big text at the top, with its win chance. Below it you get the win chance for all three calls and a short reason why.

Decision tree: the square is your call. Each circle is a chance event, like converting or not, or the kick being good or not. The probability is on the branch. Each triangle shows your win chance at the end. A call's win chance is the branch probabilities times the end values, added up. The calls we did not pick are crossed out. The punt has no circle because the model uses the average punt from that spot.

Best call by yards to go: this strip shows the best call at your field position for every yards to go, so you can see where the answer changes. Gray means it is a close call (less than 1 percentage point apart), so either is fine. Tap a box to jump to it.

How it works

We set it up as a dynamic program, like in class. Each possession left in the game is a stage. The situation at your 4th down (score, field position, yards to go) is the state. Go for it, field goal, or punt is the decision. We work backward from the end of the game, where a win is 1, a tie is 0.5, and a loss is 0, and at every 4th down we pick the call with the highest win chance.

Three examples

Example 1: Tied, 4th and 1, your own 41 to 45

Put in: 4 possessions left (about 6 to 8 minutes left), Tied, your own 41 to 45, 1 yard to go.

Best call: Go for it. It wins by 3.8 percentage points.

Win chance: go for it 49.3%, punt 45.5%, field goal 28.4%.

Example 2: Down 3, 4th and 6, opponent's 30 to 34

Put in: 2 possessions left (about 2 minutes left), Down 3, opponent's 30 to 34, 6 yards to go.

Best call: Field goal. It wins by 2.5 percentage points.

Win chance: field goal 22.7%, go for it 20.1%, punt 0.0%.

Example 3: Up 3, 4th and 4, your own 31 to 35

Put in: 2 possessions left (about 2 minutes left), Up 3, your own 31 to 35, 4 yards to go.

Best call: Punt. It wins by 7.0 percentage points.

Win chance: punt 70.8%, go for it 63.7%, field goal 37.2%.

What we assume

There is one 4th-down call per possession. Both teams play like an average NFL team the rest of the time. Punts go the average distance for that spot. After a score or a kickoff, the next drive starts at the 30. After a drive with no score, the other team starts where drives from that spot usually hand the ball over. You know how many possessions are left. A tie at the end counts as half a win, which is our stand-in for overtime. The chances come from league-wide data, not from the two teams actually playing.

Data: nflfastR play-by-play, 2018 to 2025 regular seasons.
